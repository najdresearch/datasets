# Prepared dataset permission requests

These requests are drafts. No maintainer has been contacted by this workflow. The historical release remains unchanged. A reply must identify the granting authority, covered dataset/revision, permitted acts, conditions and intended-use scope before it closes an evidence entry.

## Common request

**Subject:** Data license clarification and permission for Najd Benchmark

Hello,

I am Mazen Alotaibi from Najd Research. We are documenting the provenance and permissions of our public Arabic/Saudi evaluation dataset, Najd Benchmark: https://huggingface.co/datasets/najdresearch/najd-benchmark.

The selected rows identified below are already included in our historical release. We could not establish applicable redistribution terms from the source card and license paths we checked. Could you point us to the applicable data license, or confirm in writing whether you are authorized to grant permission for us to:

1. Retain and publicly redistribute these selected questions, answers and labels with source attribution through Hugging Face and reproducible collection scripts.
2. Normalize their format and use them to evaluate models and publish benchmark results for research.
3. Separately, use the same data in paid Evaluation-as-a-Service, where clients receive private evaluations and failure analysis. Please explicitly confirm or exclude this commercial use.

Please state the required citation/notices, any restrictions or third-party material exclusions, and whether the permission covers the historical publication as well as future use. If you are only hosting the data and cannot grant these rights, please direct us to the rights holder. We will preserve the terms you provide with the source provenance.

Thank you,
Mazen Alotaibi — Najd Research
https://najdresearch.com
