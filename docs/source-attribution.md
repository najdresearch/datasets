## Remaining action

The 12 sources marked `permission_evidence_missing` cover 2,759 historical rows. Checked public cards and common license paths did not establish redistribution terms. Targeted searches of the local project recovery files and Drive found historical rights queues and owner-attestation records, but no additional permission documents. Absence from these searches is not proof that permission does not exist.

To close each entry, record an applicable data license or permission covering the selected data and intended redistribution. Alternatively, exclude its case IDs from a separately versioned successor with an explicit removal manifest and new evaluation scores. Do not silently change the historical dataset or carry its scores to a different case set.

Research purpose and citation alone do not establish redistribution permission. Noncommercial and share-alike terms must retain their scope; this register does not approve commercial EaaS use. The nine web-source entries record the owner’s explicit confirmation, not independently examined permission documents.
